## Apps

### 🔮 AURIC — Breathwork & Focus
> *breathe · focus · live*

A minimalist approach to mindfulness. Science-backed breathing cycles designed to help you reclaim focus in a world of constant noise.

**Features:**
- 15 science-backed breathing exercises
- Multi-user breathing sessions
- Global leaderboard — compete on consistency
- Fully offline-ready

---

### 🔦 LUMIO — Clean Flashlight
> *light · utility · clarity*

A flashlight app for people who want more than a toggle. No ads. No bloat. Just beautiful, functional light.

**Features:**
- Torch with brightness control
- Strobe with adjustable speed
- SOS emergency flash
- Morse code encoder

## Philosophy

> Good software doesn't shout. It just works.

Every app I build follows three rules:

1. **Zero bloat** — if it doesn't serve the user, it's gone
2. **Intentional design** — every screen has a reason to look the way it does
3. **Offline-first** — your phone, your data, no excuses
